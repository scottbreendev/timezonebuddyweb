What is a world clock app?

A world clock app lets you see the current local time in multiple cities and countries at once — without mental arithmetic or switching between apps. Whether you're collaborating with a team across continents, keeping in touch with family abroad, or managing calls across different time zones, a good world clock app keeps everyone's time visible at a glance.

Time Zone Buddy goes further than a basic world clock. Each zone card shows daylight arcs, sunrise and sunset times, work hours indicators, and UTC offsets — all updating in real time. It's the world clock app that tells you not just what time it is somewhere, but whether people there are awake, working, or asleep.
